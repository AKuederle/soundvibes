Diagnosis: reinitializing the upstream thread pool after a completed decode
crashed a newly created native worker in GDB. Initialize pools once, serialize
native calls, and retain libraries for process lifetime because their worker
threads persist after model handles are destroyed. Corrected Hann coefficient
rounding to match Torch's window construction; punctuation now matches too.
